Our Expertise

Our Business Areas

Diversified operations spanning mining, industrial logistics, agriculture, construction, and strategic investments across Tanzania and beyond.

Core Operations

Primary Business Areas

Our core focus remains on the mining and mineral value chain, from exploration to market.

01

Mineral Exploration

Systematic geological surveys, prospecting, and exploration for gold, diamonds, copper, silver, and other precious or semi-precious minerals across Tanzania's rich mineral belts.

02

Mining Operations

Acquisition, leasing, and development of mining concessions, claims, and licenses. Responsible extraction using modern, environmentally conscious mining techniques.

03

Mineral Processing

Establishment and operation of mineral processing plants, crushing facilities, and refineries connected with mining activities to add value before export or local sale.

04

Industrial Logistics

Comprehensive transport, haulage, freight forwarding, and logistics solutions for goods and passengers by road, rail, water, and air across Tanzania and internationally.

05

Supply Chain Support

End-to-end supply chain management services including procurement, warehousing, inventory management, and distribution for mining and industrial operations.

06

Strategic Industrial Investments

Evaluation and investment in opportunities that align with our long-term strategy, creating diversified value for shareholders and stakeholders.

Diversified Portfolio

Extended Business Activities

As outlined in our Memorandum of Association, Fortenous Metals is positioned to operate across multiple complementary sectors.

07

Petroleum & Fuel Stations

Sale, distribution, importation, exportation, and storage of fuel, petrol, diesel, gas, lubricants, tyres, tubes, and related petroleum products.

08

Agriculture & Farming

Farming, horticulture, livestock keeping, dairy farming, poultry, bee keeping, fish farming, and processing of coffee, tea, cashew nuts, cereals, and other crops.

09

Food & Beverage Processing

Manufacturing and processing of food products, beverages, fresh juices, mineral water, soft drinks, sauces, and operation of grain mills and bottling plants.

10

Hospitality & Tourism

Hotels, motels, lodges, guest houses, safari camps, restaurants, bars, and tour operating services for Tanzania's thriving tourism sector.

11

Construction & Civil Works

Civil engineering, construction, building, renovations, plumbing, interior decoration, landscaping, and maintenance of commercial and residential properties.

12

General Trading & Retail

Import, export, wholesale, and retail of hardware, building materials, electronics, textiles, furniture, groceries, and general merchandise.

13

Automotive & Equipment

Buying, selling, importing, exporting, and repairing motor vehicles, motorcycles, tractors, industrial machinery, mining equipment, and spare parts.

14

Education & Training

Establishment and management of educational institutions, vocational training centres, professional courses, and entrepreneurship training programs.

15

Real Estate & Property

Real estate development, property management, construction of commercial buildings, residential houses, apartments, and shopping malls.
