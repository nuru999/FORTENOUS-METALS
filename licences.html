Before we publish

What to collect from your dad.

Ask for clear PDF scans or high-resolution photos of the front and back of every current document. Also ask which licence belongs to which company, project, mineral, and location.

Expired, transferred, cancelled, third-party, or application-only documents must be labelled honestly and should not appear as active Fortenous Metals licences.

  • Full licence or permit name and document type
  • Exact holder name shown on the document
  • Issuing authority and issue date
  • Expiry date or current status
  • Mineral, licence area, region, and project name where applicable
  • Written confirmation that the company may publish a redacted copy
Publication rules

What will be shown—and what will stay private.

A polished compliance page should increase confidence without handing strangers a complete pack of sensitive corporate documents.

Public summary

Document name, authority, high-level scope, status, relevant dates, and a partially masked reference.

Redacted copy

Signatures, national IDs, private addresses, personal contacts, QR codes, payment data, and security features removed or covered.

Due-diligence copy

Complete records shared directly with qualified parties through an agreed process, possibly after confidentiality protections.

Corporate documents

The memorandum is a foundation—not a licence.

The company memorandum confirms the legal name, private-company structure, share capital, initial directors, and authorised objects. It allows the company to pursue mining, mineral processing, mineral trade, logistics, and other activities.

It does not by itself grant a mining right, environmental approval, export permit, financial-services licence, or permission to start every listed activity.
