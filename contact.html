For faster review

What a strong introduction contains.

You do not need to send a full data room. A concise, credible first message is enough to decide whether the discussion should continue.

01

Your identity & mandate

Organisation name, country, website, your role, and whether you are acting for yourself or another rights holder.

02

The opportunity

Commodity or service, general location, current stage, legal or licence position, and the documents available for review.

03

The specific request

Explain whether you need capital, technical support, equipment, operations, off-take, due-diligence material, or a management meeting.

Document requests
