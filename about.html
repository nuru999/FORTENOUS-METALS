Company Profile

Company Overview

A Tanzanian Company with African Ambitions

Fortenous Metals Tanzania Company Limited is a private company incorporated under the laws of the United Republic of Tanzania. The company was established to participate in the exploration, development, processing, and responsible utilization of mineral resources while supporting industrial growth and economic development.

In addition to its mining activities, the company is positioned to operate in complementary sectors that strengthen its long-term sustainability and business resilience. Fortenous Metals is committed to conducting its operations with professionalism, transparency, operational excellence, environmental responsibility, and a strong focus on health and safety.

The company strives to become a trusted partner in Tanzania's mining and industrial sectors by embracing innovation, building strategic partnerships, and creating sustainable value for all stakeholders.
