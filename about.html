Company profile

A focused Tanzanian platform for responsible growth.

Fortenous Metals Tanzania Company Limited was formed as a private company limited by shares, with constitutional authority spanning the mineral value chain and other lawful business sectors.

Who we are

Local understanding. Partnership mindset.

Fortenous Metals is developing its position in Tanzania’s mining and industrial landscape. The company’s memorandum authorises mineral exploration, mining, processing, mineral trading, acquisition of mining rights, and the establishment of related facilities.

Authorisation in a memorandum defines what a company may pursue; it does not prove that every activity is currently operating or licensed. Fortenous Metals therefore presents only verified corporate facts publicly and will add specific project and licence claims as supporting records are approved.

Credibility principle: every public claim should be supported by a regulator-issued document, an approved project record, or a verified company statement.

Strategic direction

What the company is working to become.

These statements express the company’s strategic direction. They are not quotations from the memorandum and should be reviewed by management as the business matures.

Vision

To become a trusted Tanzanian mineral-development company known for disciplined partnerships, responsible project execution, and lasting local value.

Mission

To identify and advance viable mineral opportunities through strong governance, suitable licences, capable partners, practical technology, and responsible stakeholder engagement.

Operating principles

Integrity, safety, legal compliance, environmental responsibility, commercial discipline, local participation, and clear communication.

Responsible development

Trust is built before a project begins.

Investors and partners need more than ambition. They need evidence that the company understands the legal, environmental, technical, social, and commercial requirements of mineral development.

Legal compliance

Obtain and maintain the rights, licences, permits, and approvals required for each activity.

Safety & environment

Integrate health, safety, environmental, and community considerations into project planning.

Commercial discipline

Evaluate opportunities on credible technical information, realistic economics, and transparent agreements.

Local participation

Build local relationships and seek practical benefits for Tanzanian people, suppliers, and communities.

Qualified partners

Work with capable technical, legal, financial, and operating partners suited to each project stage.

Evidence-led communication
