Corporate foundation

Clear governance. Verifiable information.

Fortenous Metals Tanzania Company Limited is a private company limited by shares under Tanzania’s Companies Act, Cap. 212. Its constitutional documents provide for mining, mineral exploration, processing, mineral trading, and related project activities.

The website separates authorised business objects from proven licences and current operations. That distinction protects the company’s credibility and helps serious partners complete due diligence efficiently.
