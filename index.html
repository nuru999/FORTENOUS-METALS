About Us

Committed to Tanzania's Economic Growth

Fortenous Metals Tanzania Company Limited is a private company incorporated under the laws of the United Republic of Tanzania. We participate in the exploration, development, processing, and responsible utilization of mineral resources while supporting industrial growth and economic development.

Guided by integrity, professionalism, and innovation, we strive to become a trusted partner in Tanzania's mining and industrial sectors by embracing modern technologies and building strategic partnerships.
